*Beowulf* begins with the story of [Hrothgar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hrothgar "Hrothgar"), who constructed the great hall, Heorot, for himself and his warriors. In it, he, his wife [Wealhtheow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wealhtheow "Wealhtheow"), and his warriors spend their time singing and celebrating. Grendel, a [troll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troll "Troll")\-like monster said to be descended from the biblical [Cain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cain "Cain"), is pained by the sounds of joy.[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf#cite_note-23) Grendel attacks the hall and kills and devours many of Hrothgar's warriors while they sleep. Hrothgar and his people, helpless against Grendel, abandon Heorot.

Beowulf, a young warrior from Geatland, hears of Hrothgar's troubles and with his king's permission leaves his homeland to assist Hrothgar.[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf#cite_note-24)

Beowulf and his men spend the night in Heorot. Beowulf refuses to use any weapon because he holds himself to be Grendel's equal.[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf#cite_note-25) When Grendel enters the hall, Beowulf, who has been feigning sleep, leaps up to clench Grendel's hand.[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf#cite_note-26) Grendel and Beowulf battle each other violently.[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf#cite_note-27) Beowulf's retainers draw their swords and rush to his aid, but their blades cannot pierce Grendel's skin.[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf#cite_note-28) Finally, Beowulf tears Grendel's arm from his body at the shoulder and Grendel runs to his home in the marshes where he dies.[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf#cite_note-29) Beowulf displays "the whole of Grendel's shoulder and arm, his awesome grasp" for all to see at Heorot. This display would fuel Grendel's mother's anger in revenge.[\[29\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf#cite_note-30)
