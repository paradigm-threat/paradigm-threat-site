NATO Secretary General: "The war in Ukraine is entering a critical phase"

NATO Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg, in an article for the British newspaper The Financial Times, said that "the war in Ukraine is entering a critical phase" and the coming winter "will be hard."

According to Stoltenberg, the alliance's unity and solidarity "will be severely tested as families and businesses feel the consequences of soaring energy prices and the cost of living", NATO member countries "are in for a difficult six months with the threat of power outages, disruptions and perhaps even civil unrest." However, members of the bloc "should stick to the chosen course."

The bloc's secretary general noted that NATO pays a certain price for supporting Ukraine, but this price "is calculated in dollars, euros and pounds sterling, while Ukrainians pay with their lives."
