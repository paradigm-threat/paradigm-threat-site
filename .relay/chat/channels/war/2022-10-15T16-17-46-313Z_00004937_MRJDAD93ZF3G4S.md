"To become a conscientious objector, or CO, usually a man had to be a member of a Peace Church such as the Quakers, Mennonites, or the Church of the Brethren. Personal feelings were not sufficient to receive CO status. Of the 43,000 who registered as COs, 6,000 refused to cooperate with Selective Service. Those men, over 4,400 of which were Jehovah’s Witnesses, went to jail. Approximately 25,000 either enlisted or allowed themselves to be drafted but served in non-combat roles within their respective branches.
