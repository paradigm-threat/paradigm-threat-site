“If passed, HB1380/SB1111, would not allow healthcare professionals from giving a minor a vaccination without written consent from their parent or a guardian. The proposed law also requires the healthcare provider log the consent receipt of the minor.

The exceptions of this law include when:

A parent or legal guardian of the minor has provided prior written, informed consent to the vaccination.
The parental rights of each of the minor's parents or legal guardians have been terminated by a court, and all opportunities for appeal have been exhausted.
Any healthcare provider that violates this proposed law may have their license suspended, revoked or refused for renewal. Also, other disciplinary action would be allowed by law.”
