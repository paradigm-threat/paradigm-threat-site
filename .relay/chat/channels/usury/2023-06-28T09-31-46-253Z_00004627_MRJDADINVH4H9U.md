“The MIT Golub Center for Finance and Policy (GCFP) today awarded the Miriam Pozen Prize to Mario Draghi during a ceremony held at MIT. Draghi, who earned his PhD in economics from MIT, led the European Central Bank during the Global Financial Crisis and most recently served as the prime minister of Italy. The biennial prize recognizes an individual who had made outstanding contributions to the advancement of financial policy research or practice, which is the core mission of the MIT GCFP. Draghi has contributed to financial policy both as an academic and at the highest levels of international finance.
