@michael.from.canada I prefer to seek an origin story for Earth that would work for any planet in the universe. There shouldn't be some unique scenario in our solar system that caused some kind of abrupt shift. Instead, we can explain every shift, why they happened, and why they always happen as part of a natural planet birthing process. Furthermore, I believe asteroids rarely ever hit planets. The idea that craters on Moon or Earth came from asteroid collisions has been successfully debunked by simple observation - all craters are round. So we can rule out any catastrophic collisions in the past entirely. The effect of some passing asteroid could not possibly match the effects of interplanetary discharge in the Golden/Dark ages
