The best way to consume foods is raw, however very few protein dominant foods may be eaten raw.  Hemp milk is one, and the other is sashimi.  Obtaining sashimi and handling it properly is quite difficult.  The lowest toxicity fish available in the USA is wild alaskan sockeye, which is not found in sushi restaurants.  Instead most sushi restaurants have farm raised salmon which is one of the most toxic foods.
