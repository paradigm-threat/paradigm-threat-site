I worked in the food industry in UK. It was some of the top end stuff. 
European food law is strict, and labelling is also pretty good,  these sort of tactics are used in USA and Asia where the regulation are not so tight. 
Yes colours are used, but it's included in the labelling.

Biggest scandal was horse meat in cheap ready meals. They only got prosecuted for mislabeling! 

And there was other stuff going on at the time so it didn't get massive. The CEO of Tesco resigned about 6 months before it hit the headlines so it was known about, 

A couple of UK supermarket are now super strict on supply chain, we could only buy ingredients from their approved supplier lists for making their cakes, & had to have traceability records for each batch that could be used to establish farm to fork by batch number.
