“An 83-acre cornfield outside the Village of Pioneer, Ohio, in the northwest corner of the state is being transformed into a model for the aquaculture industry. AquaBounty, a land-based salmon producer, officially broke ground April 20 for its 479,000-square-foot state-of-the-art facility that will produce 10,000 metric tons of Atlantic salmon every year.

According to AquaBounty CEO Sylvia Wulf, the Pioneer site was chosen over 250 other possible locations around the country. Cost of the project will be in the $290 million to $320 million range and bring between 100 and 150 new jobs to the area. She added that commercial stocking of salmon eggs will begin in late 2023.”
