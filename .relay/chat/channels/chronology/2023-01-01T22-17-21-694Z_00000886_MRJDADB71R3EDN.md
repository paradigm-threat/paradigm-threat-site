4. Shiva in Buddhism
Shiva is a ‘deva' god in Buddhism. Devas dwell in god-realm but are bound by karma and have to perish at some point. 'Shiva' is also considered a 'dharmapala’ protector of dharma of Buddha's teachings.
5. Buddha in Hinduism
'Buddha' as incarnation of Vishnu was introduced as a tool to co-opt Buddhism in the Puranic period from 8th century onwards. This happened due to mention of Buddha as a maya-moha / deceptive avatara of Vishnu as mentioned in Agni Puran, Linga Puran, Bhagvat Puran etc.
