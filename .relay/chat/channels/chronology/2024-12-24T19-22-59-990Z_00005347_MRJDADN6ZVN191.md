"While there isn't a complete lack of evidence for the Maccabees in Israel, the main reason why there isn't a significant amount of readily identifiable archaeological evidence is because the Books of Maccabees are not part of the Jewish biblical canon, leading to less focus on excavating sites directly related to their story; additionally, many potential sites have been misidentified or misinterpreted over time due to historical confusion and religious sentiment.
